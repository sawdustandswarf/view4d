# view4d

> [!CAUTION]
> **All the code in this repository was generated by Claude, an AI from Anthropic, and has not been checked by a person.** It may have bugs or not do what it says. Read it before you rely on it, and use it at your own risk.

3D scenes that change over time, in a pane of [termmd](https://github.com/sawdustandswarf/termmd): a model that
follows its file as you rewrite it, or an orrery with the planets where they
are now.

```
view4d part.stl &                        # beside the shell; follows the file
view4d part.stl --place "fixed=top-right w=40 h=15" --focus=no &
view4d part.stl --png part.png           # a picture, anywhere
view4d case.obj &                        # OBJ: its parts in their MTL colours
view4d plate.3mf &                       # 3MF, from a slicer or CAD
view4d hinge.prim --set length=60 &      # a primlib primitive, built here at these values
view4d hinged_lid.prim --animate open_angle=0:110 &   # the lid opening and closing, every 4 s
view4d --orrery &                        # the planets now, from above
view4d --orrery --idle &                 # small, top right; the shell keeps the keys
view4d --orrery --speed 4 &              # a week a second
view4d part.stl --orbit 12 &             # the eye goes round once every 12 s, until a drag
```

Drag to orbit; right or middle drag, or Ctrl+drag, to pan (Shift+drag is
termmd's own, for selecting); the wheel zooms. `f` fits, `r` resets, `1`–`4`
are the front, top, right and isometric views, `w` the wireframe, `o` the
overlay (name, size in mm, triangles, parts; the orrery's date), `q` quits.
In the orrery, Space pauses, `+`/`-` go faster and slower (real time up to
ten years a second), `.`/`,` step a day, `>`/`<` a year, `n` is now, and `d`
changes the distance scale (log, even, true); `l` hides the names.

A `.prim` file is a primitive from [primlib](https://github.com/sawdustandswarf/primlib) (`python3 -m primlib export hinge -o
hinge.prim`): view4d builds it from its operations, at the file's values or as
`--set NAME=VALUE` gives them; a check that fails is shown instead. A recipe
(`python3 -m primlib export edge_run`) places primitives by key: `--set "key='SMF"`; an assembly
places recipes too, and turns joints: `view4d hinged_lid.prim --set open_angle=60`. Text (stencil's)
needs its font file where the file is opened.

A model of several parts (OBJ, 3MF, a primitive's compound): click a part, or `]` and `[`, to select
it; `h` hides it, `H` shows it alone, `a` shows them all. `--only NAME` and
`--hide NAME` do the same from the start; `--bed 350x350` outlines a bed of
that size under the model, and `b` toggles it.

Click the pane to give it the keyboard, elsewhere to give it back to the shell.

It is an embedded app of termmd, built on termmd's `termmd-surface` and
`termmd-vulkan` crates; nothing else. Status and steps: [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md).

## Demos

Inside termmd: `demos/orrery.sh` (the planets, a week a second) and `demos/hinge.sh` (a hinge opening and closing while the eye goes round it) and `demos/live.sh` (a model rewritten while it is shown, as view4d follows its file). Each starts a pane and returns; `PLACE` gives other placement keys and `DURATION` stops it after that many seconds. termmd's `demos/tour.sh` runs them.

## Build

Needs a stable Rust toolchain, a Vulkan driver, and termmd to show it in (`--png` works
anywhere). Cargo downloads termmd's crates from GitHub:

```sh
cargo build --release
./target/release/view4d --orrery &    # from a shell inside termmd
```

## Hardware

Designed and optimised for my laptop, a **Lenovo ThinkPad T14 Gen 3**: 12th Gen Intel Core i7-1255U, Intel integrated graphics (Alder Lake), 16 GB of memory and a 1920 × 1200 screen at 1.5× scale, running [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org) (Arch Linux and Hyprland). Other machines may well work, but they haven't been tried.
